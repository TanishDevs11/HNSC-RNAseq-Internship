## Data and resources

This work uses open-access data generated by **The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA)** Research Network, distributed through the **NCI Genomic Data Commons**, and by the **Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx) Project**, accessed through the **UCSC Xena** TCGA-TARGET-GTEx hub (TOIL RNA-seq recompute). Pathway and functional annotation came from **Reactome** and the **ToppGene Suite**. The analysis relies on the open-source **R** and **Bioconductor** communities, in particular the authors of DESeq2, sva, apeglm, clusterProfiler, ReactomePA and TCGAbiolinks.
